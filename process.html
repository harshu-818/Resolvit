How the process works

Every complaint goes through the same four stages.

  1. Register. Fill in the complaint form with the details and any supporting document.
  2. Review and assign. The complaint is checked and sent to the responsible department.
  3. Action. An officer works on the issue and posts updates.
  4. Resolution. You are told the outcome and can give feedback or ask for a review.

Status meanings

PendingReceived, waiting to be reviewed.
In progressAssigned and being worked on.
ResolvedThe issue has been fixed and closed.
